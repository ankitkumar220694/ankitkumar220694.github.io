## How I work

- **Start with the operating problem.** The model matters, but reliability, adoption, cost and measurable business impact define success.
- **Own the full path.** I work across architecture, data and model pipelines, APIs, interfaces, cloud infrastructure, delivery and monitoring.
- **Design reusable systems.** Shared patterns beat isolated wins: paved roads, observable defaults and clear escape hatches.
- **Lead with evidence.** I make trade-offs explicit, instrument outcomes and communicate the result to technical and business stakeholders.
